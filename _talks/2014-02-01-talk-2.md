I will present my research on **information-theoretic feature selection** for network intrusion detection (NID). This work addresses critical challenges in cybersecurity by identifying minimal yet maximally discriminative features, reducing computational overhead while maintaining detection accuracy. The talk will include benchmarks against industry-standard datasets (e.g., CIC-IDS2017).
